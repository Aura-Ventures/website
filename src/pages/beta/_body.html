Get it on iPhone or iPad

Uses Apple TestFlight — free, safe, and made by Apple.

iPhone & iPad

via Apple TestFlight
  1. 01Download TestFlight from the App Store — it's free and made by Apple.
  2. 02Once TestFlight is installed, come back here and tap the button below.
  3. 03Tap Accept, then Install. Aura will appear on your Home Screen with a small orange dot — that's the beta indicator.
Open in TestFlight →
Requires iOS 16 or later. If the beta is full, reach out to Brian and he'll add you manually.

Get it on Android

Android uses APK sideloading — takes one extra step, but it's straightforward.

Android

via Firebase App Distribution
  1. 01On your Android phone, go to Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps and allow your browser to install apps.
  2. 02Tap the button below to open the beta invitation and sign in with your Google account.
  3. 03Download and install the Aura APK. The app will appear on your home screen when done.
Join Android Beta →
Requires Android 8.0 or later. Trouble installing? Email support@auraofhope.com
